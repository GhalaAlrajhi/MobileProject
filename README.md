# University Hall Reservation & Maintenance App

A university mobile application designed to streamline lecture hall reservations and maintenance management for students and professors. The application improves communication between users and support staff while making hall availability, booking, and maintenance information easier to access.

## Project Overview

The application provides a centralized platform for discovering and reserving university halls, viewing hall information, reporting maintenance issues, and receiving notifications about bookings and hall changes.

It integrates **Google Maps** to help users locate buildings and halls and provides access to the university's **ODUS** website for convenient access to related services.

## Key Features

* Search for available university halls
* View hall details, including seating capacity and equipment status
* Locate university buildings and halls using Google Maps
* Book halls for students and professors
* Give professors priority when managing conflicting reservations
* Report equipment or maintenance issues with photos
* Notify users about confirmed bookings and hall changes
* Allow central support to monitor and manage maintenance requests
* Coordinate quick fixes and larger maintenance operations

## Problem Definition

The existing process of reserving and maintaining university lecture halls can be inefficient and difficult to manage. Users may experience difficulties finding available halls, reporting equipment problems, and receiving timely updates about reservations or changes.

The application addresses these challenges by providing a centralized and organized platform for hall reservations, maintenance reporting, and communication.

## Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is a mobile application that brings hall reservation and maintenance services together in one platform. Users can search for halls, view their availability and details, make reservations, and report maintenance issues.

Central support can monitor reported issues and coordinate the appropriate response, while notifications keep users informed about booking confirmations and changes.

## Technologies & Integration

* **Android Mobile Development**
* **Google Maps Integration**
* **ODUS Integration**
* Mobile UI/UX
* Notifications
* Image Upload for Maintenance Reports
